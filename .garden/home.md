## Knowledge lifecycle

New material begins in `inbox/` or enters a durable content area as `draft` or
`proposed`. Agent-generated claims stay unreviewed until a human explicitly
promotes them. Sources, confidence, counterarguments, and unresolved gaps remain
visible as the garden evolves.
